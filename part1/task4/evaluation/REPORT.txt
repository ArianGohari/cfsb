Adversarial findings and smallest next fixes
No accepted assertions occurred on the 27 negative/context/uncertainty sentences. This is mostly abstention and sparse candidates, not demonstrated safety against negation or context errors. Development-only perturbations of actual annotations correctly rejected missing mentions, shifted offsets and conflicting same-span identities. The corpus text was never fabricated or altered.

1. Measure and repair screening/alignment losses first. Preserve separate not-processed, entity-missing and candidate-supported counts; never interpret any of them as biological absence. Broader assertion retrieval and Caco2-only experimental evidence need distinct scopes.
2. Resolve entity representation before adding relation patterns. Run inputs contain gene/protein and viral-construct/cell-line identity hazards; the current schema also omits chemical regulators. Do not turn every Gene into a Protein or accept a cell-line namespace as proof of cell identity. Unsupported classes and unknown normalization must stay explicit.
3. Only after candidate coverage is available, test clause-local negation/speculation, coordinated targets and expression-versus-activity predicates on new development examples. Current whole-sentence vetoes and complete-sentence grammars are narrow (rules.py:99–179); the present held-out set cannot quantify their separate loss. Do not relax rejection guards wholesale.

Only this aggregate report should be shared for tool development. Keep reference.json, freeze_reference.py and sentence-level result details out of tuning; once those labels are inspected for development, use new held-out material. No exact test labels are reproduced here.
